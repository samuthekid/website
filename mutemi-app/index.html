MuteMi sits quietly in your menu bar and gives you one keyboard shortcut to instantly mute or unmute every microphone on your Mac — not just the default one.
NO Accessibility permissions, NO Input Monitoring, NO background daemons. The menu-bar icon turns red the moment you're muted, so you always know your status at a glance.
