How it works

Three keys. Zero windows in your way.

1

Hit the shortcut

Press ⌘⇧L from anywhere — or click Lock Now in the menu bar.

2

Input is sealed

Every keyboard and mouse event is swallowed system-wide. Your work keeps running, fully visible behind the glass.

3

Press ESC to release

Back in control instantly — or in Max mode, it locks your screen the moment you leave.

Built for people who leave the keyboard running
